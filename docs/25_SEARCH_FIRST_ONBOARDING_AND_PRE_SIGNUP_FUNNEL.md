# 25 — Search-first Ayo: pre-signup business discovery and onboarding

**Status:** founder-confirmed product direction, 25 October 2026. SPECIFICATION, NOT SHIPPED. **Supersedes** prior signup-first/Shopify-first customer onboarding in docs/07 and older Shopify alpha homepage. No HTML crawler, search provider, preview lead provider or AI API is actually online in this source.

## Product contract
Ayo is an **Africa-first e-commerce operating intelligence**, not a store software add-on. The **core entry action** is **Search for your business**. A customer can enter a *company/brand name + optional market/city*, *website URL*, or *public online storefront/social profile URL* in a single prominent search control. Support Nigeria, Ghana, and further markets via provider-independent country configuration. Signup does NOT precede discovery. Shopify is one optional integration AFTER business signup/claim, never a blocking step to learn about Ayo.

Primary funnel (one coherent product journey):

`Visitor → Enter name or public site → Disambiguate actual business → Perform bounded evidence gathering → Show truthful mini-report + (only if real search results exist) a few potential B2B opportunity previews → Primary CTA "Create free Ayo account" → verified customer account → securely claim/import that particular report → create private business workspace → optional public source verification → Ayo intelligence and real lead discovery in dashboard → optional Shopify/other integrations → merchant-authorized actions → verified results`.

Never label pre-account public research as ownership verification. Users can analyze competitors too. Personal/private connected data and outbound actions require legitimate merchant authentication and permission. The first 500 distinct customer USERS receive **all released features free** with no paid feature gates; do not demand a credit card to claim a report.

## Screen-by-screen UX
1. **Public Ayo home:** bold Ayo identity, one primary search control: "Search for your business or paste its link"; optional Nigeria/Ghana selector and `I don't have a website`. A short explanation says Ayo studies publicly accessible information and does not access private business metrics yet. No Shopify banner as primary CTA. The account/login link is secondary.
2. **Disambiguation:** for `brand in Lagos`, show candidate public businesses with **actual provider URLs**, brand, region and provider citation. User chooses or corrects. If no candidates, offer direct URL, an Instagram/public marketplace link, or describe business; **never fabricate matching results**.
3. **Live progress:** show job status `QUEUED/RUNNING/PARTIAL/SUCCEEDED/FAILED`, named stages with actual events (`discover`, `fetch`, `extract`, `evaluate`, `opportunity-search`, `report`). No fictitious progress percentages. Respect robots, site availability and platform terms. Give fast time to first value with staged output; never imply third-party sites were read if not fetched.
4. **Public mini-report:** a real business snapshot containing observed name/location/categories/visible offer, value proposition and buyer paths, public-page friction (contact, catalog, delivery, trust, messaging), SEO discoverability opportunities (only if cited), 2–5 prioritized *hypotheses* with effort/confidence, visible coverage and links to each source. Distinguish `OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN`. No revenue, conversion, inventory, profit, audience size or advertising estimates without an authorized source. Clearly label undetermined facts and incomplete coverage.
5. **Prospect teaser:** when real licensed search exists, display up to 2–3 **organization-level**, sourced potential B2B buyer/distributor/stockist candidates with an explanation of fit; `prospect discovered ≠ qualified lead ≠ contacted lead ≠ sale`. No harvested personal contact details pre-account, fake leads, unsolicited messages or automated sending. If unavailable: "Lead discovery will be available after setup" rather than fabricated previews.
6. **Conversion:** CTA **"Create your free Ayo account"** and secondary "I already have an account". Explain that signing up lets them save the mini-report, develop opportunities and connect their selling tools. Don't gate the entire existence of a useful report to force signup. Truthfully state founding cohort rules; avoid claiming remaining free slots unless an atomic count confirms it.
7. **Secure handoff:** after signup/verification, bind an eligible unclaimed report to *this* customer's new private workspace via one-time, short-expiry, scoped claim token and server-side transaction. Prevent takeover, replay, cross-user browsing, IDOR, and copied token escalation. Browser URL/session alone does not authorize site ownership. No automatic business ownership proof from public search, email matching, or user statements.
8. **First dashboard:** Welcome summary sourced from the saved mini-report; tabs Home / Ayo / Work / Grow / Business, with Website Intel, Leads, Connections as key flows. Ayo's next job is **find/qualify real opportunities** from the business's actual offer and selected countries. Merchant confirms category, target customer, area, price segment and goals; no demographic profiling/guessed payment channels. Draft-only until lawful outreach channel, merchant authority and approval established.
9. **Optional connections:** Shopify tile lives in Connections, alongside future WooCommerce, Paystack/Flutterwave and custom-site options. "Skip" is a first-class action; the rest of Ayo still works on public evidence + merchant-supplied facts, with confidence warnings. Never make Shopify mandatory for onboarding.
10. **No website at all:** Search business name + location and public, permitted business listings; optionally public Instagram/Facebook/marketplace/WhatsApp *business landing link* when provider access and terms permit. Offer manual product/category/location/offer questionnaire and optional uploads from merchant, tagged `OWNER_PROVIDED_UNVERIFIED` until verified. No attempt to scrape login-required social channels or WhatsApp conversations. Without public evidence, generate a **starting business profile** from labeled merchant-provided details, not an asserted independent web audit.

## Multiple identities and business claims
Entity-resolution table with candidate IDs, canonical domain/public profile, registered country, legal/owner claims separately. Business ownership verification is independent of anonymous website lookup, public domain attribution, platform OAuth or marketing consent. Public competitor research must never grant action scope over that competitor. Each report records its acquisition origin and whether it is independent, operator-provided or provider-authenticated.

## Analytics and research experiment instrumentation
Record `search_entered`, `candidate_chosen`, `crawl_started`, `real_source_fetched`, `report_completed`, `report_viewed`, `lead_preview_seen`, `signup_started`, `signup_verified`, `report_claimed`, `business_profile_saved`, `lead_research_started`, `shopify_connection_started`, `shopify_connected_real`. Tag each with real, pseudonymous correlation IDs; avoid PII in telemetry. Measure time-to-first-cited-fact, source coverage, pre-signup completion, report→signup conversion, retained reports, legitimate leads per claimed report, false claims and provider cost per successful discovery. Never fabricate a funnel event.

## Acceptance criteria
- A new anonymous visitor can reach **a sourced mini-report without Shopify and without creating an account**, when public search and fetch providers are configured. No search vendor → honest `search_not_configured`; direct URL may work only with secure egress. No public source → accurate incomplete state.
- `brand` with several matches requires user selection; a mistaken match cannot become a verified owned business.
- A missing website must not cause signup failure or fake a scraped website. The owner can provide their own facts with source provenance.
- A visitor cannot inspect another visitor's unclaimed report with guessed IDs; public and private scopes differ.
- Claimed report survives new account flow; token replay/expiry/foreign account fail.
- No products, financial metrics, named people, invented B2B organizations, purported conversions or "leads contacted" unless external evidence actually exists.
- The first 500 USER accounts have access to every actually released Ayo research and growth capability, without premium gates.
