# Ayo — Africa-first e-commerce intelligence (founder direction)
**Status:** Founder-confirmed direction, engineering requirements proposed for implementation. **Updated:** 2026-10-25.

## The product, not a comparison
**Ayo is Ayo.** It is one unified intelligence built to understand and help grow actual e-commerce businesses, with special attention to how commerce works across African markets. Do not market Ayo as a version of, clone of, or substitute for any named competitor. Other companies' features can provide functional research references only. E-commerce businesses are the initial market; do not implement physical retail operations in V1.

### Founder-confirmed commitments
- **Initial users:** African e-commerce businesses, starting with Nigeria and Ghana; architecture permits country expansion without writing bespoke products. Shopify first, but Ayo can research websites even before connecting Shopify. Other e-commerce/website platforms, African payments, and eventual Ayo-native storefront are later integrations/capabilities.
- **Interaction:** One coherent Ayo intelligence, routing user needs to approved specialist tools invisibly. Don't force merchants into an agent marketplace to do one job.
- **Revenue model:** First 500 distinct legitimate customer *users* receive every released Ayo feature free with no paywalled tools or operators; new features remain included. Later-user pricing and lifetime legal language require founder decisions.
- **Quality:** A real product with real sign-in, real owned-business data, sourced website intelligence, qualified prospect discovery, and at least one safely executable approved action at first substantive merchant release. No fake business results, integrations, AI activity, leads or outreach.
- **Sales:** Ayo should find **real potential business leads**, research them, build an evidence-backed opportunity queue, draft culturally and commercially appropriate pitches, and track actual outcomes through authorized systems.

## The differentiation is operational, not cosmetic
Ayo's advantage should be tested in actual local merchant workflows:
1. **WhatsApp/social-led buying:** Some merchants operate through DMs, WhatsApp catalogs, social storefronts and links rather than a formal checkout. Understand that pattern, but do not imply that scraping private social conversations is authorized. Use official integrations and merchant permissions.
2. **Locally correct payment semantics:** NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR as native values; bank transfer vs Mobile Money vs card; pending transfers aren't received revenue. Webhooks, fees, refunds, settlement timing, and reconciliation vary. Never convert a transaction's currency to another silently.
3. **Commerce trust signals:** published phone/WhatsApp contact, business name, clear delivery/refund policy, address/service region, payment method availability, local currency, product descriptions, stock and mobile usability. Findings must be factual or explicitly a recommendation, not insinuations about reputation.
4. **Regional sales discovery:** businesses, distributors, resellers, creators, retail stockists and wholesale buyers, but sourced to public **business-level** evidence; never promise verified demand or outreach consent merely because a phone number is public.
5. **Country-aware execution:** opt-ins, language/tone, timezones, messaging limits, compliance and provider support chosen from an explicit country capability pack, not guessed from a customer's IP address or accent.
6. **Small-business affordability:** all features free for first 500 user accounts, with transparent independent provider cost/anti-abuse safeguards; low-cost batch crawling, explicit LLM usage budgets, time-to-first-value, progressive enhancement even with no Shopify.
7. **Founder-forward accessibility:** simple mobile-first onboarding, clear plain-English explanations, WhatsApp-style conversation familiarity as UX choice without impersonating WhatsApp, data-minimal onboarding, resilient slow connectivity and retries.

## Example merchant journeys with acceptance tests
### NIGERIA — social + Shopify seller
The merchant creates a business (Nigeria/NGN; actual locale chosen), enters their public shop URL, gets a cited website audit. They install Ayo's Shopify app separately and consent to approved scopes. Ayo reconciles real order and catalog records, distinguishes paid from pending bank transfer, and identifies listing improvements grounded in inventory/price evidence. A proposed Shopify update requires an immutable preview, merchant approval, server-side authorization and read-after-write verification. If Shopify is disconnected, Ayo still offers only sourced public analysis.

### GHANA — boutique looking for wholesale accounts
Merchant enters products/categories/areas/country, Ayo finds public business leads in Accra or Kumasi, deduplicates company domains, shows visible products/contact channels and reason-fit with source URLs. Lead status starts **RESEARCHED**, never CONTACTED. Ayo drafts a tailored pitch that can mention mobile money only if supported by the merchant and provider capability pack. No WhatsApp/email is sent until a lawful communication route, approval and provider integration exist. Replies/deals are only recorded on actual receipt or explicit merchant input.

### Cross-market growth
A merchant opens a Ghana and a Nigeria store. Country+currency+timezone belong to each commerce location/store. Ayo doesn't sum GHS+NGN to a meaningless 'sales total'. If FX conversion is offered, it requires a rate source, timestamp and original amounts.

## Product analytics and quality indicators (instrument only when real)
- Verified merchant signup and workspace creation funnel; proportion reaching first cited site report.
- Public-research precision: cited support for facts; provider/source freshness, coverage and recrawl failures.
- Lead quality: company/domain duplicates, validated source contact channels, merchant 'useful' feedback (NOT fabricated reply rates).
- Real Shopify OAuth success, sync completeness, reconciliation error, per-shop timezone/currency correctness.
- Draft-to-approval conversion and executed-to-provider-confirmed conversions by action type.
- Actual outcomes: replies, meetings, orders or revenue from authorized, reconciled sources; no AI-attributed uplift without a credible methodology.
- Country cohort by verified merchant-selected business country, not inferred ethnicity or citizenship.

## Non-negotiable copy and UX policy
'Ayo found 12 candidate businesses with publicly cited sources' is acceptable when true. 'Ayo found 12 customers' is not. 'Suggested campaign drafted' must not look like 'campaign launched'. 'Sales opportunity' is never 'revenue recovered'. Every card has source, owner/permission, timestamp, status and a route to challenge/correct the result.
